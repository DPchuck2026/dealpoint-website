05 / ABOUT

Meet Chuck Crumpton.

Chuck Crumpton

Founder & CEO, Strategic Advisory Forum
Founder & Special Advisor, Medpoint

“My team will work like hell to get you the best price with the best terms. You’ve worked too hard to leave money on the table.”

Chuck is the founder and CEO of Strategic Advisory Forum. Guided by honesty, integrity, and a love for teaching others, he brings an owner’s perspective to every engagement: he has built and sold three companies of his own and understands the complexity of preparing a business for market.

He founded Medpoint in the toolshed of his garage in 2002 and now serves as its Founder and Special Advisor. The global quality assurance, regulatory, and clinical affairs consultancy grew to more than 350 employees and consultants serving medical device and pharmaceutical companies across the United States, Europe, Asia, and Latin America.

Before Medpoint, Chuck held a senior management role with a Fortune 15 company now part of Verizon, where he led the initiative in lowering employee attrition from 73% to 18%, saving the company millions each year.

  • Undergraduate studies: BJU
  • Graduate studies: Cornell University

He is also a published author, podcaster, and keynote speaker at events in the United States, Europe, and Asia.
